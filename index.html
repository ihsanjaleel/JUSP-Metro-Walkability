About this viewer

A working map for choosing Namma Metro stations for footpath surveys. It shows what kind of place each station serves and how much of its surroundings people can actually reach on foot. It is not an official map.

Station types

Each station has one primary type, based on two sources of evidence: ridership (who uses the station, and when) and land use within its 800 m walking catchment. Interchanges and other features are listed as tags. Types marked provisional rest on OpenStreetMap sites, because the 2017 land-use map does not cover the station.

Sources

  • Ridership: BMRCL hourly station data released under RTI (OpenCity), September 2025. Figures are average daily entries plus exits, on weekdays (21 days, excluding the 5 September holiday) and weekend days (8 days). The Yellow Line had been open only a few weeks. The Pink Line (opened 13 November 2026) has no data yet.
  • Walking catchments: ArcGIS Pro Service Area analysis, 800 m along OpenStreetMap streets and paths (Geofabrik, March 2026), from station entrances (BMRCL GTFS, unofficial). Motorways, flyovers and vehicle underpasses are excluded.
  • Land use: KSRSAC urban land use 2017 (via OpenCity), within the former BBMP area. It has no mixed-use class, so mixed use is inferred.
  • Supporting evidence: OpenStreetMap office and tech parks, hospitals, colleges and shops.
  • Lines and stations: BMRCL GTFS (Vonter/bmrcl-gtfs, OpenStreetMap-based, ODbL). The Pink Line is drawn schematically. S.G. Palya is not mapped.
  • Corporations: GBA five-corporation map, September 2025.
  • Master plans: RMP 2015 via MapWarper; draft 2031 hosted by the Master Plan Viewer project.
  • Base maps: © OpenStreetMap contributors; imagery © Esri.
