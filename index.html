About this viewer

A working map for the metro station footpath survey. It shows ridership, how much of each station's surroundings people can reach on foot, and the main roads to be surveyed. It is not an official map.

Footpath survey

Seven stations are surveyed: Benniganahalli (pilot), Indiranagar, Yeshwantpur, Jayadeva Hospital, Pattandur Agrahara, Infosys Foundation Konappana Agrahara and Madavara. Central stations (within 5 km of Majestic) are skipped because Tender S.U.R.E. works are done there. In the inner (5–12 km) and outer (beyond 12 km) areas, the busiest station on each line was picked, plus Indiranagar for its sub-arterial roads. Each station card says why it was picked.

The survey covers the footpaths on arterial, sub-arterial and collector roads within a 500 m walk of the entrances, plus extension roads. An extension is added where, 500–800 m away, there is a bus stop with at least 100 weekday departures on routes that do not stop within 500 m, or a large site (2 ha or more) with no gate within 500 m.

Sources

  • Ridership: BMRCL hourly station data released under RTI (OpenCity), September 2025. Figures are average daily entries plus exits, on weekdays (21 days, excluding the 5 September holiday) and weekend days (8 days). The Yellow Line had been open only a few weeks. The Pink Line elevated section (opened 14 November 2026) has no data yet.
  • Walk areas: ArcGIS Pro Service Area analysis, 500 m along OpenStreetMap streets and paths (Geofabrik, March 2026), from station entrances (BMRCL GTFS, unofficial). Motorways, flyovers and vehicle underpasses are excluded. Pink Line catchments were recomputed the same way from corrected station points.
  • Road types: OpenStreetMap road class: trunk and primary = arterial, secondary = sub-arterial, tertiary = collector. Footpath length counts both sides; a divided road drawn as two one-way lines counts one side per line.
  • Bus stops: BMTC timetable (Vonter/bmtc-gtfs, unofficial, November 2026). Large sites: OpenStreetMap.
  • Tender S.U.R.E. roads: Jana Urban Space portfolio; matched to OpenStreetMap by name. Cantonment Road and Planetarium Road could not be matched.
  • Land use: KSRSAC urban land use 2017 (via OpenCity), within the former BBMP area. It is shown for background only.
  • Lines and stations: BMRCL GTFS (Vonter/bmrcl-gtfs, OpenStreetMap-based, ODbL). Pink Line alignment and station points from OpenStreetMap (March 2026), with the station names adopted in November 2026.
  • Corporations: GBA five-corporation map, September 2025.
  • Master plans: RMP 2015 via MapWarper; draft 2031 hosted by the Master Plan Viewer project.
  • Base maps: © OpenStreetMap contributors; imagery © Esri.
